# 📦 Supply Chain Analysis Dashboard \| Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing supply chain data to identify
operational patterns, evaluate performance, and generate actionable
business insights.

The analysis covers product performance, supplier efficiency, inventory
levels, manufacturing costs, transportation expenses, and product
quality using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The objective is to help businesses understand their supply chain
performance and identify areas that require improvement.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

-   Analyze revenue and product performance.
-   Compare supplier efficiency and lead times.
-   Examine inventory and stock levels.
-   Evaluate transportation and logistics costs.
-   Identify product quality issues and defect rates.
-   Generate actionable recommendations for better supply chain
    management.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

-   **Microsoft Power BI** -- Data visualization and dashboard
    development.
-   **Power Query** -- Data preparation and transformation.
-   **DAX** -- KPI calculations and measures.

## 📈 Key Analysis & Business Insights

### 1. Revenue Performance by Product Type

-   Skincare generates the highest revenue.
-   Haircare ranks second.
-   Cosmetics contributes the lowest revenue.

### 2. Supplier Lead-Time Analysis

-   Supplier 3 has the longest average lead time.
-   Supplier 1 has the shortest average lead time.
-   Supplier delivery performance varies across suppliers.

### 3. Transportation Cost Analysis

-   Road transportation has the highest shipping cost.
-   Air and Rail have similar cost levels.
-   Sea transportation has the lowest shipping cost.

### 4. Product Quality & Defect Analysis

-   Haircare has the highest defect rate.
-   Skincare has a slightly lower defect rate.
-   Cosmetics has the lowest defect rate.

### 5. Revenue Contribution by Supplier

-   Supplier 1 contributes the highest revenue.
-   Supplier 4 contributes the lowest revenue.
-   Supplier performance should be evaluated using both revenue
    contribution and operational efficiency.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard provides a consolidated view of:

-   Overall revenue and sales KPIs.
-   Revenue by product category.
-   Products sold by product type.
-   Stock levels by product type.
-   Average manufacturing cost.
-   Supplier lead-time comparison.
-   Shipping cost by transportation mode.
-   Defect rate by product type.
-   Revenue contribution by supplier.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1.  **Improve Supplier Management:** Closely monitor suppliers with
    longer lead times.
2.  **Focus on Quality Improvement:** Investigate the causes of higher
    Haircare defects.
3.  **Control Transportation Costs:** Review high-cost transportation
    options and compare alternatives.
4.  **Monitor Supplier Performance:** Evaluate suppliers based on
    revenue contribution and lead-time performance.
5.  **Maintain Focus on Strong Products:** Continue effective planning
    for high-performing categories such as Skincare.

## 🏁 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Power BI can transform supply chain data
into meaningful business insights.

The analysis highlights supplier efficiency, logistics costs, product
quality, and revenue performance as important areas for management
attention.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of supply chain operations
and supports better data-driven business decisions.
